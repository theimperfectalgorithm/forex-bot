==========================================================================================
Methodology: shuffle the ORDER of the actual historical trade set (same trades, same P&L
values, same eventual total -- order-invariant by construction), recompute the equity curve
and max drawdown / max losing streak for each shuffled order. This produces a distribution
of PATH-DEPENDENT outcomes (drawdown, streaks) consistent with the historical trade
population -- it does NOT prove future profitability, only characterizes the range of
drawdown/streak outcomes this specific set of historical trades could plausibly have produced
under a different arrival order.
