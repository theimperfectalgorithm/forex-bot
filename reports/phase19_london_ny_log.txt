which London session, preserving each session's own marginal range/percentile
distribution and the pair/year grouping -- only the SAME-DAY LINKAGE is shuffled. This
tests whether the same-day pairing itself carries information beyond each session's own
independently-realistic volatility distribution (unlike shuffling raw values, which would
