PART 12 -- NULL / RANDOMIZATION TEST
==========================================================================================
Methodology: for each pair, we do NOT randomize trade outcomes (there is no trade yet).
Instead, we bootstrap-resample a random sample of the SAME SIZE as the event group,
drawn from all OTHER bars in the SAME VOLATILITY-REGIME TERCILE (matching the event
group's volatility composition), 2000 times, and compute the mean forward-ATR
return each time -- this builds a null distribution of what "a typical bar in the same
vol regime" produces by chance, controlling for the fact that events cluster in certain
vol regimes. We then report the percentile rank of the REAL event-group mean within that
null distribution: a low percentile means the event mean is unusually negative (reverts
more than chance), which is evidence the down-move itself carries information.
