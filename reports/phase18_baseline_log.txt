PART 1 -- FROZEN SHAPE DEFINITIONS
  body_ratio        = |close-open| / (high-low)
  close_location    = (close-low) / (high-low)
  lower_wick_ratio  = (min(open,close)-low) / (high-low)
  upper_wick_ratio  = (high-max(open,close)) / (high-low)
  shape_score = close_location - body_ratio  (composite, frozen BEFORE this backtest --
    body_ratio+upper_wick_ratio+lower_wick_ratio=1 identically, so these 4 quantities are
    not independent; phase17 found close_location (d=+0.101) and body_ratio (d=-0.107) to
    be the two cleanest, least-redundant Tier-1 effects, so the composite uses only those two.
  Threshold = the POOLED HISTORICAL MEDIAN of shape_score across the full research sample,
    computed ONCE before any trade simulation -- not searched for backtest performance.
  Mechanics (identical across A/B/C, unchanged from phase15): entry = next candle open,
  stop = 1.0xATR, target = 1.0R, max hold = 4 bars (60min), SL priority on same-bar touch.
